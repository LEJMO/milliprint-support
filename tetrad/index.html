Frequently asked

  • How do I set a date quickly? Type it: “email Kim friday 10am”, “pay rent every month”. Use #tag and @name too.
  • How do I sync between iPhone, iPad and Mac? Tetrad Pro › Settings › iCloud sync, then restart the app on each device.
  • How do I connect Apple Reminders? Board menu › Edit board › Link a Reminders list.
  • How do I cancel my subscription? Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your iPhone. Lifetime is a one-time purchase and never renews.
  • I bought Pro on another device. Settings › Restore purchases.
