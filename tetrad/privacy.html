Tetrad – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Summary

Tetrad does not collect, store or transmit your personal data to us. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising.

Your tasks

Boards, tasks, notes, steps, tags, attached photos and focus history are stored in a database on your device. If you turn on iCloud sync, they are also stored in your private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit), which only your Apple ID can read. We cannot access it.

Apple Reminders and Calendar

If you link a board to a Reminders list or turn on calendar events, Tetrad reads and writes those items on your device through Apple's EventKit framework, with your permission. Nothing is sent to us.

On-device intelligence

Sorting suggestions and step-splitting run on your device (Apple Intelligence where available, otherwise built-in rules). Task text is not uploaded.

Photos

When you attach a photo or add tasks from a photo, you pick the image with the iOS photo picker. Tetrad does not request access to your whole photo library. Text recognition runs on your device.

Purchases

Tetrad Pro is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple processes payment; we do not receive your payment or personal details.

Exports

CSV files, images and backups are created on your device and shared only where you choose.
