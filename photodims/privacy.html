PhotoDims – Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Summary

PhotoDims does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising.

What the app stores

Photos you add, the dimension notes you draw on them, project names and captions are stored only on your device, inside the app's own storage. They are included in your device backups (iCloud or computer) according to your iOS settings. The developer has no access to them.

Camera and photo library

The camera is used only to take a photo you choose to add to a project. The photo picker runs outside the app; PhotoDims only receives the photos you select and never reads your library. When you choose "Save to Photos", the app asks for permission to add the exported image to your library.

Purchases

PhotoDims Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Apple's App Store. The app never sees your payment details. Purchase status is verified on the device through StoreKit.

Sharing and export

Files you export (JPG, PDF, backup zip) leave the app only when you share them through the iOS share sheet to an app or destination you choose.

Data deletion

Deleting a photo, a project or the app removes the corresponding data from your device. Since nothing is stored elsewhere, there is nothing else to delete.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not collect any data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with an updated date.
