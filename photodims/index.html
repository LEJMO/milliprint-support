PhotoDims – Support

PhotoDims puts the dimensions you measured right on your photos. Take a photo, draw a dimension line across the part you measured with your tape, type the value (fractions like 3 1/2 work), and share it as a JPG or PDF. Whoever receives it needs no app.

PhotoDims does not measure anything. You measure — the app records the numbers where they belong: on the photo. Check every measurement yourself before you build, order or sell.

FAQ

Does the app measure the object for me?
No. There is no AR or automatic measurement. You type what your tape or laser meter says. That is the point: the numbers on the photo are yours, not an estimate.

Where are my photos and notes stored?
Only on your device. There is no account and no cloud of ours. To move to a new phone, export a backup (Settings › Export backup) and restore it on the new device. Restoring is always free.

What does Pro include?
Unlimited projects (free: 3), exporting a whole project as numbered JPGs or one PDF, and the backup file. One-time purchase — no subscription, no ads. Nothing you unlock is ever locked again.

I bought Pro but it isn't active.
Open Settings › Restore Purchases. You need to be signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy.

The receiver can't open my file.
PhotoDims exports only standard JPG and PDF files. They open in any photo viewer, mail app or browser.

Can I edit a note later?
Yes. Notes are stored as vectors on top of the original photo — the photo itself is never modified. Tap a note in the editor to select it, drag the handles to adjust, tap the value to change it.
